0. Original: the current Barnyard look (mock-up)

What it is: the dashboard as it is today, rebuilt from the site's own values (shell.css, tiles.css, themes.js) on the shared example data, so you can set it beside the others. It is a faithful approximation, not a screenshot of your signed-in pages (those were not viewable here).
Structure: a left sidebar with the brand mark, line icons, groups (Work, Systems), counts as pills; a big hero headline ("Three things need you.") at up to 48 px with tight tracking and a dim sub-line; tiles ("Waiting on you", "Since you last looked", "In progress and soaking", "This week") as rounded cards with soft shadows; an accent rail on each needs-you row; a dotted spine for the feed.
Type and icons: Bricolage Grotesque for headings, IBM Plex Sans body, Plex Mono for figures; a line icon beside every menu item.
Colour and backgrounds: 18 themes (light and dark each), amber default; 18 CSS-only backgrounds, strength, density and sidebar options.
The tells that bother you (from the audit): the giant hero sentence; decorative gradient backgrounds and the amber-plus-violet pairing; every block a rounded panel with the same shadow; chatty headings ("Waiting on you", "Since you last looked", "In progress and soaking"); the matched font-and-icon kit; fade-and-rise motion.
Controls in the mock-up: theme, light or dark, background, strength, density. Ops board is the second view.
