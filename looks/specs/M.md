# Look M: Lab Book

Mock-up: `M-lab-book.html` (preview picture: `M-lab-book-preview.png`). Generated by `_gen-MNO\build.ps1` from the site's real themes (themes.js), backgrounds (shell.css) and fonts (the repo's self-hosted files, embedded as data in the mock-up only).

## Rationale
An IT administrator's day is a log: things opened, checked and closed, each with an age. A lab notebook is the form that already has dates, a margin for states and a place to paste in a table. Lab Book makes time the organising axis. The left margin reads down the page as a timeline of ages and states ("2 days", "today", "5 days", "done 01:00"); the body says what. The only emphasis is something drawn on the page: a ring, a wavy underline, a tick, an exclamation mark. So anything marked by hand means "look here", and nothing is coloured by category. Tables are used only where the data is a table (systems, the week) and are pasted in as taped strips, so they read as evidence attached to the day, not as the main surface.

## Layout rules (what replaces rounded cards and accent rails)
- One ruled sheet per page, with a margin column on the left (148px, 124px compact) and a spine with punched holes. No cards, no panels inside the page, no accent bars.
- Every entry sits on a rule line. Row height is one rule (30px, 24px compact); headings are one or two rules.
- Section titles are plain words with an underline, not boxes.
- Tabular data goes on a taped strip (a slightly tilted sheet with two tape pieces). Nothing else is tilted.
- Navigation is index tabs on the right edge of the page (Overview, Ops board, Monitoring, Calendar, Projects, Vulnerabilities). The current tab is joined to the page and bold.
- State marks, all plain CSS: an empty box (open), a ticked box with struck text (done), a ring (overdue date or the sentence's key phrase), a wavy underline (a system that is down or a risk in running text), a half-filled box (running), a dashed box (backlog), an exclamation mark in a ring (overdue).

## Type
- Titles: Bricolage Grotesque 600 (already on the site).
- Notes and body: IBM Plex Sans 400 and 500, 15px on a 30px rule (13.5px on 24px compact).
- Margin dates, ages, ids and system names: IBM Plex Mono 400 (the margin and the ids only, not decoration).
- No handwriting font: the hand feel comes from CSS marks (rings, wavy underline, slight rotation), not from a typeface.

## Icon approach
No icons. State is drawn with CSS borders and shapes (the marks above) and always has its word next to it ("up", "down", "degraded", "due 28 Nov"). Nothing relies on colour alone.

## Tone of voice (5 rewrites)
| Today | Lab Book |
|---|---|
| Waiting on you | Open items (4 open) |
| Since you last looked | Log, since 09:00 |
| In progress and soaking | Running |
| Services | Systems, checked 22:51 |
| The board is private. Log in | Sign in to open this notebook. |
Rules: a dated note, not a headline; sentence case; the age goes in the margin, the action in the body; say what is wrong in the sentence ("APP-02 has not answered for 14 minutes").

## AI clichés avoided
Cream-and-terracotta serif (the paper takes its colour from the chosen theme, never fixed cream), dark with one acid accent, gradient wash, rounded cards with accent rails and soft grey shadows, big number in a tile, all-caps labels above headings, numbered markers (the margin carries real dates and ages, not 01/02/03), emoji, middle-dot joined meta strings, centred layout.

## Backgrounds
The page sheet is opaque, so a background shows on the desk around the book (widest on a large screen, hardly visible on a phone).
- Suit: Plain, Dots, Grid, Blueprint, Contours, Horizon, Glow, Aurora, Mesh (soft colour behind the paper reads as a desk).
- Clash: Ledger (a second ruled paper with its own red margin line outside the page, doubles the effect), and Stripes, Lattice, Scales, Beams at strong strength (a busy desk next to a ruled page). They still work; they just fight the page.

## What it would change in the shared shell
- New setting `look` (here `labbook`), validated in themes.js like the others, applied as `data-look="labbook"` on `<html>`; it travels in the bh_prefs cookie and the profile sync with the rest of the look-and-feel keys.
- shell.css gets a layer scoped under `[data-look="labbook"]` (about 150 lines: page, margin column, spine, strip, tabs, marks). The 12 colour tokens and the status tokens are used unchanged.
- shell.js renders the menu as edge tabs instead of the sidebar when the look is on (same menu items, same links). The Settings panel gets a Look choice.
- overview.js and the other pages need a "notes" render variant: each widget renders as dated rows (margin cell + body) instead of tiles. Widgets that are inherently graphical (week strip, Nasdaq) render as a taped strip. This is the main cost.
- Ops board list view maps directly to rows; the Ops board column view would be hidden in this look.

## Sidebar setting
`full`: tabs show their words (vertical). `icons`: tabs show initials only (O, Ops, M, C, P, V), 38px wide. On a phone the tabs become a row of normal tabs above the page.

## Contrast (all 18 themes x light/dark = 36 combinations, tokens from themes.js, computed with `_gen-MNO\contrast.ps1`)
No pair fails 4.5:1. Lowest ratios: text on page 11.81 (Steel dark), strong text on page 15.71, margin and dim text on page 5.57 (Arctic light), dim text on strip 5.20 (Arctic light), "up" text on strip 4.91 (Stone light), "down" text on strip 5.20 (Stone light), tab text 10.92 (Navy dark). The accent token is never used as text (Amber light would give 4.38). Decorative marks (rings, ruling, tape) are not text; the rings use strong text colour at 72% opacity, which keeps them above 3:1 against the page.

## Open doubts
The ring and wavy marks are drawn with CSS borders on inline text, so they can wrap across a line break (the mock-up keeps the ring on one line with no-wrap; a very long phrase may need to wrap). Rotated strips can look slightly soft on low-resolution screens. Pages with inherently wide tables (Vulnerabilities) will need the strip to scroll sideways.
